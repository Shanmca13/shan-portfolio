05 / Ask about my work

Questions a hiring manager might ask.

This page shares how I approach common delivery challenges. It is a static portfolio page, not a live AI chatbot.

How do you improve sprint predictability?

I start with the team's own delivery data, clarify the sprint goal, improve work readiness, and look for recurring sources of carryover. The team uses retrospectives to test small changes and review whether those changes help.

How do you handle blockers?

I make the blocker visible, clarify its impact and owner, and help the right people connect to resolve it. I follow up without taking ownership away from the team or turning the conversation into blame.

How do you work in a SAFe environment?

I help teams understand iteration goals in the context of broader planning, make dependencies and risks visible, and keep communication flowing across product, engineering, and delivery stakeholders.

What is your leadership style?

Clear expectations, calm facilitation, data-informed conversations, and shared accountability. My role is to help the team solve problems and improve its way of working.
