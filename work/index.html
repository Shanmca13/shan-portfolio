02 / Selected work

Small improvements. Visible outcomes.

Representative delivery-improvement examples, described at a high level to protect client and employer confidentiality.

Delivery predictability

Make sprint commitments more reliable

Improve planning conversations, refine work readiness, and review delivery trends with the team instead of treating estimates as promises.

Reported outcome: predictability improved from 72% to 90%.
Workflow discipline

Strengthen Jira hygiene

Set clearer expectations for work item updates and use a consistent review rhythm to make progress, risks, and dependencies easier to understand.

Reported outcome: compliance improved from 60% to 95%.
Reporting efficiency

Reduce manual status effort

Standardize the information collected from teams and focus reporting on meaningful status, next steps, risks, and decisions required.

Reported outcome: reporting effort reduced by 40%.
Scaled delivery

Support alignment across teams

Use planning, dependency conversations, and shared visibility to help product, engineering, and delivery stakeholders stay aligned around outcomes.

Approach: transparent risks, shared ownership, continuous improvement.
